# Tester guide

Thanks for testing the Foster Cat Care Guide. It takes about 15–20 minutes. Please use your phone if you can, since most fosters will.

Don't worry about getting things "right." If something is confusing, that's exactly what we need to know.

## Before you start

- Note your device and browser (for example, iPhone Safari or Android Chrome).
- Note your foster experience: new, some experience, or very experienced.

## Tasks

Try each task without help. For each one, note whether you finished it, how easy it felt (1 = very hard, 5 = very easy), and anything that confused you.

1. **Browse:** You're fostering a 5-week-old kitten. Find out what vaccines it needs and when.
2. **Urgent:** Your adult male foster cat keeps going to the litter box but nothing comes out. Find out what to do.
3. **Feeding:** You have a bottle baby who weighs 180 grams. Find out how much formula to give per feeding.
4. **Tracking:** Add two weights for a kitten named "Test": 200 g yesterday and 195 g today. What does the app tell you?
5. **Stool:** Your kitten's poop looks like pudding. Find out whether you should worry.
6. **Search:** Use the search box to find information about ringworm.
7. **Household:** Find advice on how your 8-year-old could help care for fosters.
8. **Sources:** Open any health topic and find where the information comes from.

## Questions afterward

1. What was the most useful part?
2. What was confusing or hard to find?
3. Was anything missing that you'd need as a foster?
4. Did any advice seem wrong or different from what your organization tells you? (Experienced fosters and vet staff: this is especially valuable.)
5. Would you use this app? Why or why not?
6. How would you rate it overall, from 1 to 5?

## How to send feedback

Use the **Send feedback** link at the bottom of the app, or open a new issue in the GitHub repository using the "Tester feedback" template. You can also send your notes directly to the person who invited you.
